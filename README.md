            <h2>Introduction</h2>
            <p> Introduction
                Welcome to the Worker Training Program, a specialized initiative aimed at enhancing skills, improving workplace safety, and empowering workers with hands-on experience. Our program is designed to help individuals develop industry-relevant skills that lead to better job opportunities and professional growth.
                
                <li>Our training covers:</li>
                <li>✔ Safety procedures to prevent workplace accidents</li>
                <li>✔ Equipment handling for effective and efficient work</li>
                <li>✔ Emergency response training to handle crises effectively</li>
                <li>✔ Workplace efficiency techniques for better productivity</li>
                
                This program ensures that every worker gains the expertise needed for a safe and productive work environment.</p>
        </section>

        <div class="module">
            <h3>✅ Safety and Hazard Awareness</h3>
            <ul>
                <li>Learn how to identify and minimize workplace hazards.</li>
                <li>Understand safety gear and protective measures.</li>
                <li>Fire prevention, first aid, and handling hazardous materials.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
        
        <div class="module">
            <h3>✅ Equipment Handling</h3>
            <ul>
                <li>Hands-on training on using industrial machinery and tools.</li>
                <li>Proper handling of heavy equipment to prevent accidents.</li>
                <li>Regular maintenance and troubleshooting of common issues.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
        
        <div class="module">
            <h3>✅ Emergency Response</h3>
            <ul>
                <li>Learn fire safety protocols and evacuation procedures.</li>
                <li>First aid training for injuries, burns, and electrical shocks.</li>
                <li>Crisis management and workplace accident response.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
        
        <div class="module">
